# Paper page - How Do Large Language Models Acquire Factual Knowledge During Pretraining?

## Abstract

LLMs acquire factual knowledge during pretraining through a process involving memorization, generalization, and forgetting, which is influenced by factors like data volume, batch size, and data duplication.

Despite the recent observation that [large language models (LLMs)](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=large%20language%20models%20%28LLMs%29) can store substantial factual knowledge, there is a limited understanding of the mechanisms of how they acquire factual knowledge through [pretraining](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=pretraining). This work addresses this gap by studying how LLMs acquire factual knowledge during [pretraining](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=pretraining). The findings reveal several important insights into the dynamics of [factual knowledge acquisition](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=factual%20knowledge%20acquisition) during [pretraining](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=pretraining). First, counterintuitively, we observe that [pretraining](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=pretraining) on more data shows no significant improvement in the model's capability to acquire and maintain factual knowledge. Next, there is a [power-law relationship](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=power-law%20relationship) between training steps and [forgetting](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=forgetting) of [memorization](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=memorization) and [generalization](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=generalization) of factual knowledge, and LLMs trained with duplicated training data exhibit faster [forgetting](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=forgetting). Third, training LLMs with larger [batch size](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=batch%20size)s can enhance the models' robustness to [forgetting](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=forgetting). Overall, our observations suggest that [factual knowledge acquisition](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=factual%20knowledge%20acquisition) in LLM [pretraining](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=pretraining) occurs by progressively increasing the probability of factual knowledge presented in the [pretraining](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=pretraining) data at each step. However, this increase is diluted by subsequent [forgetting](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=forgetting). Based on this interpretation, we demonstrate that we can provide plausible explanations for recently observed behaviors of LLMs, such as the poor performance of LLMs on [long-tail knowledge](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=long-tail%20knowledge) and the benefits of [deduplicating the pretraining corpus](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=deduplicating%20the%20pretraining%20corpus).
